L-Touch is an interactive 3D sales platform for real estate developed by LUNAS. Buyers explore a digital twin of a residential project and browse its units.

L-Touch platform overview.

As a Senior Unreal Engine Developer I build and maintain the UE5 configurators behind it: architecture, C++ plugins and editor tools, networking, optimization, and support of legacy products.

Contents

    3D scenes, art, and UI design were created by the team. My part is the development work described below.

    Data-driven Configurators

    I architected the configurators to be data-driven, so they scale to projects with thousands of units. This includes the camera system, unit filtering and listings, CRM integration, and Arabic right-to-left localization.

    Ayla Oasis, a project built on L-Touch.

    C++ Plugins and Editor Tools

    I develop C++ plugins exposed to Blueprints, along with editor tools for artists and designers.

    Networked Kiosk

    A two-machine kiosk: a touch-screen controller and a wall display. I built the networking that keeps their state in sync over TCP and UDP.

    Optimization

    I own CPU optimization on Windows and Apple Silicon Macs. On the heaviest projects, I reduced game thread time from 80 ms to 3 ms by reworking tick-heavy systems, components, and UI.

    Legacy Support

    Maintaining legacy products and migrating them to the latest Unreal Engine versions, including fixing packaging, plugin, and cross-platform build issues.
